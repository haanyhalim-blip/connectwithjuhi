# Juhi's business card

- `Juhi-business-card-print.pdf` – send this to the printer. Two pages (QR side, photo side), 91 × 61 mm each: a standard 85 × 55 mm card plus 3 mm bleed on every side, so the printer trims it to size.
- `card-qr-side.png`, `card-photo-side.png` – pictures of each side, for checking or sharing.
- `card-source.html` – the design itself. Open it in Chrome and print to PDF (paper size 91 × 61 mm, no margins, background graphics on) to make a new PDF after a change.

The QR code opens https://connectwithjuhi.com.
